# EDULEDGER — DEVICE INSTALLATION GUIDE

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for installing EduLedger Progressive Web Apps on **Android**, **iPhone**, **iPad**, **Windows**, **macOS**, and supported desktop browsers.

---

## 1. Android (Google Chrome & Microsoft Edge)

### For School Administrators:
1. Open **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** on your Android smartphone or tablet.
2. Visit `https://eduledger.co.in`.
3. An installation banner reading **"Install EduLedger App"** will appear automatically at the bottom. Tap **Install**.
4. If the banner does not appear, tap the browser menu (three vertical dots `⋮` in the top right corner) $\rightarrow$ tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
5. Confirm by tapping **Install**. The EduLedger icon will appear on your home screen and in your Android app drawer.

### For Teachers:
1. In Chrome, open `https://eduledger.co.in/teacher`.
2. Tap the bottom banner **"Install EduLedger Teacher App"** or tap `⋮` $\rightarrow$ **Install app**.
3. Confirm installation. The dedicated Teacher App will launch full-screen with no browser address bar.

### For Parents:
1. In Chrome, open `https://eduledger.co.in/parent`.
2. Tap **"Install EduLedger Parent App"** or tap `⋮` $\rightarrow$ **Install app**.
3. Confirm installation. Parents can now access attendance, fees, homework, and exam results directly from the home screen icon.

---

## 2. Apple iOS & iPadOS (Safari on iPhone & iPad)

Apple iOS does not support the automatic installation banner found in Android. Use the following standard 6-step setup:

1. **Step 1: Open Apple Safari**: Launch the official **Safari** browser (Chrome or Firefox on iOS cannot install PWAs to the home screen due to Apple iOS restrictions).
2. **Step 2: Navigate to Target Portal**:
   * For Administrator: `https://eduledger.co.in`
   * For Teacher: `https://eduledger.co.in/teacher`
   * For Parent: `https://eduledger.co.in/parent`
3. **Step 3: Tap the Share Button**: Tap the **Share** icon (a square with an upward-pointing arrow) at the bottom of your iPhone screen (or top of your iPad screen).
4. **Step 4: Select "Add to Home Screen"**: Scroll down the share sheet and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** (next to a plus icon).
5. **Step 5: Confirm by Tapping "Add"**: You will see the app name and icon preview. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
6. **Step 6: Open from Home Screen**: Tap the EduLedger icon on your home screen. It will open in standalone mode with full screen, native-like navigation.

---

## 3. Windows 10 & Windows 11 (Google Chrome & Microsoft Edge)

1. Open **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. Visit `https://eduledger.co.in` (or `/teacher`, `/parent`).
3. In the browser address bar (top right, near the star bookmark icon), click the **Install** button ($\oplus$ icon or computer monitor icon).
4. Click **Install** in the popup prompt.
5. EduLedger will open in its own clean application window without browser tabs or toolbars. You can pin it to your Windows Taskbar and Start Menu.

---

## 4. macOS (Chrome, Edge & Safari 17+)

### Using Google Chrome or Edge on Mac:
1. Open the portal in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click the **Install** icon in the address bar $\rightarrow$ click **Install**.
3. The app is added to your macOS Applications folder and Dock.

### Using Safari on macOS Sonoma (14+) / Safari 17+:
1. Open the portal in **Safari**.
2. In the top macOS menu bar, click **File** $\rightarrow$ **Add to Dock...**.
3. Click **Add**. The app launches as a standalone Mac application from your Dock.

---

## 5. Troubleshooting & FAQ

* **Q: Why don't I see an Install button on iPhone Chrome?**  
  *A: Apple strictly requires PWAs on iOS to be installed via Apple Safari using the "Share $\rightarrow$ Add to Home Screen" feature.*
* **Q: Does EduLedger consume large storage on my phone?**  
  *A: No. EduLedger is an ultra-lightweight Progressive Web App that consumes less than 5 MB of local device storage.*
* **Q: Will the app update automatically when new features are released?**  
  *A: Yes. Whenever a new version is deployed, the in-app update banner notifies you and updates instantly with a single tap.*
